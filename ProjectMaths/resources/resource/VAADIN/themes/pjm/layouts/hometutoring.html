Home Tutoring
Our Home Tutors, will call to your home at a time that is convenient for your lesson.

The tutor will initially assess the student's maths aptitude and decide with you a plan for completing the syllabus.

The year consists of four terms each of a ten lesson duration.

The student will have access to our Project Maths site and can avail of all the benefits this entails below.
  • A Question and Answer interactive service.
  • A Student forum allowing communication between students.
  • All lesson/ notes are published after each tutoring session is completed.


Student will log in with their username and password assigned to them on enrolment. Home tuition costs 40 euro per lesson.

A twenty five percent deposit is required upfront at the beginning of each term.

The balance is paid per lesson.
