Academy Tutoring
Our Academy structure is based on a maximum of ten students per classroom.

All classrooms are equipped with overhead projectors and white boards.

The year is broken out into four terms, each of a ten week lesson duration.

On enrolment the student will be furnished with a user name and password for our Project maths Academy website.

The website is an interactive tool and comes with.
  • A Question and Answer interactive service.
  • A Student forum allowing communication between students.
  • All lesson/ notes are published after each tutoring session is completed.


At enrolment each student must pay for the full term of ten lessons up front. Payment is on a term basis.
